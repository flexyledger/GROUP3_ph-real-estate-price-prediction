📍 Location Dominance (62.06%)

Location accounts for over 62% of price variance, affirming that Philippine real estate is heavily anchored by localized geographic prestige and neighborhood convenience.

📐 Structural Geometry (32.52%)

Floor Area (24.78%) is the strongest structural feature, followed by Land Area (7.74%). Together with location, they account for over 94% of property valuation influence.

🎯 Log-Target Transformation

Trained using log1p(Price) to normalize heavy positive skewness in real estate transactions, eliminating outlier distortions across high-value luxury tiers.

Technical Protocol: In accordance with course guidelines prohibiting deep learning and AutoML, three traditional models (Linear Regression, Decision Tree Regressor, and Random Forest Regressor) were compared under strictly identical train/test splits (80/20) and 5-Fold cross-validation. Preprocessing transformers were fit strictly on the training partition to eliminate data leakage.
